Off-Manifold Refinement

Guiding Video Generators with a Frozen World Model

Use the world-model signal before the video is finished.

OMR uses masked-future predictive surprise from frozen V-JEPA 2.1 to correct a frozen video generator during a single sampling trajectory. A 33.3M generator-specific adapter keeps guidance in latent space—without in-loop VAE decoding or a V-JEPA encoder pass.

Method

World-model guidance in generator-latent space.

Video generators can produce convincing clips that violate basic cause and effect. OMR turns a frozen world model’s predictive temporal prior into a local correction, connecting the two models only through a separately trained representation adapter.

Frozen generatorFrozen world model33.3M adapterNo in-loop decode–encode

zt+Δt ← zt + Δt · uθ(zt, t) − η(t) · ∇ẑ₁ EV-JEPA(Aψ(ẑ₁))

The frozen generator supplies the ODE velocity; the frozen predictor supplies a local surprise correction through the adapter.
  1. 01

    Predict

    At a guided ODE step, estimate the provisional clean video latent from the current noisy state.

  2. 02

    Measure surprise

    Map that latent into V-JEPA feature space, mask future tokens within a 48-frame window, and compare them with the predictor’s forecast.

  3. 03

    Refine

    Backpropagate predictive surprise through the adapter and apply the normalized correction to the generator’s velocity field.

Why it is efficient

A shorter gradient route.

WMReward-∇ differentiates through a V-JEPA encoder and VAE decoder. OMR instead uses the predictor and generator-specific adapter, avoiding those two additional Jacobians and their compute.

The comparison supports a useful direct correction; showing that gradients are larger or better preserved would require gradient-norm measurements.

Representation alignment

Why can an adapter connect independently trained models?

The generator latent and world-model target describe the same video under different objectives: one preserves content and motion for synthesis; the other preserves structure for future prediction. OMR learns a task-specific transformation between them without claiming that the two spaces are identical or globally aligned.

Operational correspondence. The adapter is trained to preserve V-JEPA token geometry; our fidelity evaluation separately tests predictive-surprise ordering. This is consistent with the intuition that representations of the same video can be mapped, but does not establish universal equivalence.

Deployment-time check. The adapter is trained on real-video latents but operates on provisional generated clean predictions—not raw noisy states. We therefore compare it with the exact decode–encode path where OMR is actually applied.

Scope and outlook

A practical interface, not a guarantee of physical correctness.

Current scope

OMR inherits the blind spots and data biases of its world model. It adds runtime and memory over base sampling, and each new VAE latent space currently requires adapter retraining. The transfer pilots are small and do not include paired prompt-level confidence intervals.

Next questions

Shared multi-resolution adapters, longer videos, complementary physical predictors, adaptive schedules, larger repeated-seed studies, and causal gradient diagnostics are promising directions. OMR can also be paired with a small final selection budget.
